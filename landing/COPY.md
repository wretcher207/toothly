## 4. Features

### Weighted like the real exam
Every question in Toothly is tagged to DANB's own domain breakdown for General Chairside, Radiation Health and Safety, and Infection Control. Chairside Dentistry runs half the GC exam, so it's half of what you practice, not a seventh of it because that's how many chapters the textbook gave it. You study in the same proportions DANB tests in.

### Spaced repetition that knows when you're about to forget
Toothly runs on FSRS, a spaced repetition algorithm that schedules each question to come back right before you'd naturally forget it, not on a fixed daily count. Get one wrong, it comes back sooner. Get it right, it backs off. A readiness score across all three exams tells you when you're actually ready to book your test date.

### Stays useful after you pass
Pass the exam and Toothly doesn't go in a drawer. The same content becomes a chairside reference: instrument lookup, procedure checklists, patient scripts, all pulled from what you already studied. It's the prep app that becomes your chairside reference.

## 6. FAQ

**What's actually on the CDA exam?**
Three exams bundled together. General Chairside (95 questions), Radiation Health and Safety (75 questions), and Infection Control (75 questions). 245 questions total.

**How long is the CDA exam?**
195 minutes if you sit for all three at once. General Chairside is 75 minutes, Radiation Health and Safety is 60, Infection Control is 60.

**What does it cost to sit for it?**
$450 for the bundled application fee, paid to DANB directly. That's separate from anything Toothly charges.

**Who's eligible to take it?**
Infection Control and Radiation Health and Safety have no eligibility requirements, anyone can register for those two. General Chairside needs either a CODA-accredited program or 3,500 verified hours of work experience.

**What's on the Infection Control exam?**
Four domains, per DANB's outline: preventing disease transmission (20%), preventing cross-contamination (34%), instrument and device processing (26%), and occupational safety protocols like the OSHA Bloodborne Pathogens Standard (20%). No eligibility requirements to sit for it, which is why Toothly is building that question bank first.

**When does Toothly launch?**
It's in development now. Waitlist members get first access and the founding $79/year price when it opens.
